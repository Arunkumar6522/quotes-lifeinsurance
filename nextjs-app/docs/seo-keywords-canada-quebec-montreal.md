## Strategic Notes for Montreal / Bilingual Market

1. **Hreflang is critical.** Montreal searches arrive in both `en-CA` and `fr-CA`. Each language variant must have its own URL with correct `hreflang` tags so French and English pages don't compete against each other in search rankings (a common Montreal SEO pitfall).

2. **Don't translate — transcreate.** Quebec French users search `soumission assurance vie` not `devis assurance vie` (European French). Terms like `assurance maladies graves` (not `assurance maladie grave`) and `assurance vie temporaire` (not `assurance terme`) match local vocabulary.

3. **Newcomers / immigrants is a high-opportunity niche.** Montreal has one of the highest immigrant populations in Canada. Keywords around `life insurance for newcomers` / `assurance vie nouveaux immigrants` have lower competition but strong commercial intent.

4. **Mortgage protection is a major trigger.** First-time homebuyers in the 30–45 age bracket actively search `mortgage life insurance` / `assurance vie pour prêt hypothécaire`. This is a strong entry-point keyword with immediate transactional intent.

5. **Google Business Profile optimization.** Local keywords (#31–34 above) directly feed the Google Maps 3-pack. An optimized GBP with reviews in both languages will capture significant zero-click impressions for Montreal/Laval searches.

6. **No-medical products are a differentiator.** National aggregators focus on fully-underwritten plans. A broker highlighting `no medical exam life insurance Quebec` / `assurance vie sans examen médical` can capture a niche with less competitive overlap.

7. **CPC context.** Insurance keywords are among the most expensive in Google Ads ($20–$60+ per click). Strong organic rankings for even a handful of these terms can save thousands in ad spend monthly, making SEO ROI exceptionally high in this vertical.
